ABOUT ME

Harnessing the Power of Data to Innovate and Inspire

Hello, I’m Anubhav Yadav, a data science practitioner and AI automation specialist based in Mohali, Punjab, India. I specialize in identifying hidden patterns in complex datasets, building predictive ML pipelines, and designing production-ready automated systems.

With hands-on experience in analytical storytelling, model evaluation, and modern AI architectures like RAG and agentic workflows, I bridge the gap between raw unstructured data and high-impact business decisions.
